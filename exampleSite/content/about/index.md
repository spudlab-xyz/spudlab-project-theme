## A project-first structure

The landing page introduces one tool rather than an organisation or catalogue. Its calls to action lead visitors directly to installation and documentation.

## Built on the Spudlab design

The typography, palette, spacing, responsive behaviour, and accessible theme control follow the visual language of the main Spudlab Hugo theme.
